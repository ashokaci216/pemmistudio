About Pemmi Studio

Pemmi Studio is a Vashi-based nail, beauty and makeup studio in Navi Mumbai, created for clients who love clean finishing, detailed work and a personal beauty experience.

We specialise in handcrafted nail art, nail extensions, bridal and party makeup, facials and beauty services. Every service is done with care, hygiene and attention to detail, so you feel confident, comfortable and beautifully prepared for your special moment.
